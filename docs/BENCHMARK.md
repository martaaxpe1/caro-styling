# Benchmark: wardrobe and AI stylist apps (October 2026)

A quick desk benchmark to decide what to borrow and what to leave out. Most 2026 roundups are written by app makers or affiliate sites, so treat feature claims as leads to check, not facts. The next step is hands-on: install two or three, add five real items, screenshot what feels good.

## The apps

| App | What it is good at | Worth borrowing | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whering | The biggest free digital closet. Cut-out photos, "Dress Me" outfit shuffle, daily pick, wishlists, lookbooks, plan outfits for events, wardrobe analytics (retire under-loved pieces). | Background removal on capture. The shuffle as a playful way to get unstuck. Wishlist as a separate space from the wardrobe. | Social, marketplace and resale layers you do not need. |
| Acloset | Fastest setup. Auto-tagging from the photo. | Let AI fill in the tags, you just confirm. | Free tier capped at 100 items, with ads. |
| Alta | AI stylist that builds a look from your own closet for a plan ("job interview", "coffee with friends"), aware of weather and calendar. Avatar try-on. | Styling by occasion is the closest match to your four occasions. A short "why this works" line. | Avatar try-on is heavy and off-purpose for you. |
| Stylebook | Manual, detailed cataloguing. Outfit calendar, wear stats. One-time purchase, iPhone only. | Wear log and stats, later. | Manual-heavy; feels like admin. |
| Cladwell | Capsule wardrobe templates, daily suggestion by weather and style preferences, chat. | Capsule thinking as a possible lens for the style profile. | Reviewers say outfits often do not go together and feel generic (stock imagery, generic chat answers). |
| Indyx | Free closet app with paid one-to-one human stylists on top. | Nothing specific; confirms people value a human-feeling "why". | AI suggestions described as basic. |
| Fits AI | Outfit builder where AI fills missing slots from your wardrobe. Weekly suggestions of new items, explained by which of your pieces they pair with. | The "pairs with N things you own" argument is exactly your key-piece logic. | Generic shopping suggestions, not tied to your stores. |
| FreshFit | Takes wardrobe, preferences and inspiration images, returns outfits and shopping suggestions for missing pieces. | Closest to your inspiration flow. | Broad shopping, nearby stores rather than your brands. |
| Outfit Maker | "Steal the look": upload an outfit you like plus a selfie, see it on you. | The name and framing of "steal the look". | Try-on, not recreation from your wardrobe. |

## What to borrow

1. **Cut-outs on capture.** Background removal makes every outfit look like a clean flat lay, and makes the wardrobe pleasant to browse.
2. **AI tags, human confirms.** Category, colour, material, season, formality and occasion are suggested; you confirm in a quick review queue.
3. **Styling by occasion.** Your four occasions become the spine of the app, not a filter buried in a menu.
4. **Shuffle.** A light, playful way to see combinations you would not think of.
5. **Key-piece argument.** A missing piece is only worth buying if it pairs with enough of what you own. Show that number and the outfits it unlocks.
6. **Under-loved pieces** (later). Wear log and "you have not worn this in 4 months".

## What to leave out

- Social feeds, followers, likes, resale marketplace.
- Avatars and virtual try-on.
- Generic shopping feeds, ads, affiliate pushes.
- Stock imagery. Always your real photos.
- Human stylist layer.

## Where Caro is different

- **You style, the AI only sees.** The opposite of every AI stylist app.
- **Shop your closet first.** An inspiration becomes your version before anything is bought; buying is for one key piece, in your own stores.
- **A studio, not a catalogue.** The design has to carry this. A tab bar over a grid of cut-outs looks like every closet app.
- **Four fixed occasions** shape how outfits are organised: office, working from home, night out, day out.
- **Made for a small circle.** Invite only. No growth mechanics, no paywall, no social feed.
